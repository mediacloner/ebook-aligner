  16. 16.  One particularly interesting program that attempted to solve Bongard problems was created by Harry Foundalis when he was a graduate student in Douglas Hofstadter’s research group at Indiana University. Foundalis explicitly declared that he was building not a “Bongard problem solver” but a “cognitive architecture inspired by Bongard’s problems.” The program was inspired by humanlike perception at every level, from low-level vision all the way to abstraction and analogy, very much in the spirit of Bongard’s intentions, though it was successful in solving only a small number of Bongard’s problems. See H. E. Foundalis, “Phaeaco: A Cognitive Architecture Inspired by Bongard’s Problems” (PhD diss., Indiana University, 2006), www.foundalis.com/res/Foundalis_dissertation.pdf.
